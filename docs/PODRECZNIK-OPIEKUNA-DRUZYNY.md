# Team leader guide (national delegations)

This guide is for **national team leaders** of an international olympiad run on this platform
(e.g. the International Quantum Olympiad). It is in English because the competition is. The
organiser's side is described in `PODRECZNIK-ORGANIZATORA.md` § 10b, the operator's in
`OPERACJE.md` § 28.

## 1. What a team leader does

Students of an international olympiad do **not** sign up themselves. Each country is represented by
a **delegation**; its team leader (or several team leaders) registers the students of that country.

- You see only your country's delegation: its students and its other team leaders.
- You never see students' work, marks or anyone from another country.
- If your country has more than one team leader, you all run **one shared team** with one student
  limit – any of you can register, correct or withdraw students.

## 2. Accepting the invitation

The organiser sends you an e-mail with a link (`…/delegation/accept/…`). The link is valid for
**14 days** and works **once**; if a new invitation is sent, the previous link stops working.

- **No account yet:** enter your first and last name, choose a password and accept the terms and
  the privacy notice. The account is active immediately – log in with your e-mail and new password.
- **You already have an account with this address:** log in first; you will be taken back to the
  invitation and only need to accept the terms.
- **You are logged in with a different account:** log out and open the link again. An invitation can
  only be accepted by the account with the address it was sent to.

One person can lead only one country's delegation in a given edition.

## 3. Registering students

Open **My team** in the account bar (`/delegation/`) and choose **Add student**. Enter:

- first name, last name, the student's own **e-mail address** (it becomes their login),
- date of birth, school, grade (if the competition asks for it),
- optionally a parent's e-mail address (minors will ask that address for parental consent).

The student receives an e-mail with a link. **The student sets their own password and gives their own
consents** – nobody can consent on someone else's behalf. Until then the account shows as *invited*;
afterwards as *active*. The student's country is always your delegation's country.

You cannot register an address that already has an account on the site – contact the organiser in
that case.

## 4. Limits and deadlines

- **Student limit:** each delegation has a limit set by the organiser (default 6). When it is reached,
  *Add student* disappears.
- **Registration window:** students can be added only while registration for the current edition is open.
- **Closed delegation:** when the organiser closes your delegation, the list is frozen – you can still
  see it, but not change it.

## 5. Correcting and withdrawing students

- **Correct details** – possible until the student activates the account. Changing the e-mail address
  sends a new link; the old one stops working. After activation the student corrects their data
  themselves in their profile.
- **Send the link again** – for students who have not activated their account yet.
- **Withdraw** – possible until the first stage of the competition starts. If the student has not
  activated the account yet, the account is deleted. If the student has already activated it, the
  account stays: the student is removed from your team, receives an e-mail, and the organiser decides
  what happens next. After the start only the organiser can withdraw a student.

## 5a. Medals and certificates

Medals are awarded from the final ranking: by default gold for the top 8 % of contestants, silver for
the next 17 % and bronze for the next 25 %; contestants without a medal receive an honourable mention
for at least half of the best score or for a full solution of one problem. Equal scores always receive
the same award. After the organiser announces the medals, the public pages *Medals* and *Country ranking*
are linked from the results table. The country ranking is unofficial and shows totals only.

Each student downloads their medal certificate and certificate of participation under *My certificates*
(`/me/certificates/`), in the language they chose for the website (11 languages, including Arabic,
Chinese, Hindi and Bengali). Ask your students to set their language before the organiser issues the
certificates – the language is fixed when the document is issued.

## 6. Your data

You can download or delete your own account data under *Account*. Deleting your account removes your
team leader role and your invitations; the students you registered stay in the team.
